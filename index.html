Questions people actually ask

Will this make me unable to enjoy sex?

No. The recording targets frequency and intrusiveness of urges, not the capacity for arousal or pleasure. The goal is that sex stops being something that interrupts you and goes back to being something you choose.

Is it permanent?

No, and it isn't designed to be. Suggestions fade without reinforcement. If you stop listening, your baseline returns over a few weeks. If you want the effect to hold, you keep a maintenance listen every week or two.

Does hypnosis actually work?

Hypnosis has a decent evidence base for pain, anxiety, and habit change, and a suggestible minority of people respond to it strongly. There is no clinical trial evidence for hypnosis specifically reducing libido — I'm not going to pretend otherwise. What I can tell you is what it is: a structured relaxation and suggestion session aimed at where your attention goes by default. Some people are highly responsive to that. Some aren't.

Can it do anything I don't want?

No. Hypnosis is not control — you can stop the recording, ignore a suggestion, or fall asleep through the whole thing. Suggestions that conflict with what you actually want don't take.

Is this a kink product?

No. There's a large erotic hypnosis scene that makes recordings on adjacent themes for arousal. This isn't that. There is no sexual content in the audio.
